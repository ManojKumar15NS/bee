03
Enzymatic Alchemy • Step 03

Living Enzymes &
Hive Trophallaxis

Inside the warm hive, foragers transfer raw nectar mouth-to-mouth to processor bees in a chain known as trophallaxis. Natural invertase and glucose oxidase enzymes hydrolyze complex sucrose into simple, bioavailable glucose and fructose, infusing the honey with a powerful antibacterial shield.

⚗️ Active Invertase 🛡️ pH 3.9 Natural Shield ✨ Antibacterial H₂O₂
04
Evaporative Capping • Step 04

Wing Fanning &
Pure Beeswax Seal

Bees distribute the nectar into hexagonal comb cells. Thousands of ventilator bees coordinate rapid wing flapping at 240 beats per second, driving 35°C (95°F) airflow to dehydrate moisture down to under 18%. Abdominal wax glands then produce virgin beeswax flakes to hermetically seal each cell indefinitely.

⚡ 240 Hz Wing Velocity 🍯 <18% Ripe Moisture 🔒 100% Hermetic Wax Seal
